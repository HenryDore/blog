# Packaging

Brighton and Hove have finally expanded the items they collect for recycling to include plastic cartons & tetrapacks. This got me thinking about our *choices* when it comes to packaging. We should choose to reduce waste wherever possible, and reduce the weight of packaging, and prefer materials that can be recycled or composted, or prefereably not have any packaging at all.

When it comes to supermarket fresh fruit and vegetables we have a few choices:
| Material | Recyclable? | Compostable? | Weight |
|---------|-------------|----------|----------|
| Paper bag | Yes | Yes | Low |
| Plastic bag | Sometimes | No | Low |
| Cardboard container | Yes | Yes |Medium |
| Plastic carton | Sometimes | No | Medium |
| Tetrapack | Sometimes | No | High |
